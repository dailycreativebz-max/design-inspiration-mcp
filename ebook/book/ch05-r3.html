Black Bean and Corn Salsa Salad

Serves 3 · 7 min
BudgetHigh-protein
Ingredients
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1 tsp taco seasoning (optional)
  • Tortilla chips or crackers
Preparation
  1. Mix beans, corn and salsa in one bowl.
  2. Season to taste; serve with chips.

Subs: Kidney beans or pinto beans; canned tomatoes with green chiles instead of salsa.

Method
No-Cook
Water
None (draining only)
Fuel
No-cook
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Filling and shelf-driven; sodium varies widely by salsa brand — check labels. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Peanut Butter Tortilla Roll-Ups

Serves 2 · 4 min
Kid-friendlyBreakfastEvacuation-readyLow-waterBudget
Ingredients
  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 6 tbsp peanut butter
  • 4 tbsp honey or jam
  • 1/2 cup raisins or trail mix (optional)
Preparation
  1. Spread tortillas with peanut butter, then honey or jam.
  2. Sprinkle with raisins, roll tightly and slice if desired.

Subs: Seed butter for nut-free; crackers instead of tortillas.

Method
No-Cook
Water
None
Fuel
No-cook
Dishes
0 — from package

Storage & safety: Excellent car-kit meal — survives heat better than most bars. Peanut allergy: seed butter. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Sardine Toast with Mustard

Serves 1 · 3 min
High-proteinEvacuation-readyLow-water
Ingredients
  • 1 tin sardines (in water or olive oil)
  • 6 crackers or 2 slices crispbread
  • Mustard or hot sauce
  • Crushed red pepper (optional)
Preparation
  1. Open tin, drain lightly if preferred.
  2. Place sardines on crackers; finish with mustard.

Subs: Mackerel, tuna or smoked oysters; lemon juice instead of mustard.

Method
No-Cook
Water
None
Fuel
No-cook
Dishes
0 — from package

Storage & safety: Single-serve tins mean zero leftovers. Fish allergy: use canned chicken. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Pantry Antipasto Plate

Serves 2 · 6 min
Evacuation-readyHigh-protein
Ingredients
  • 1 cup crackers
  • 1/2 cup canned olives, drained
  • 2 oz jerky
  • Shelf-stable cheese or cheese spread
  • Canned pepperoncini or roasted red peppers, drained
Preparation
  1. Arrange everything on one plate or board.
  2. Serve crackers alongside.

Subs: Swap any element for what you stock: canned artichokes, dried fruit, nuts.

Method
No-Cook
Water
None
Fuel
No-cook

Storage & safety: A build-your-own plate children accept easily. Sodium is high across the plate — budget water. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.
