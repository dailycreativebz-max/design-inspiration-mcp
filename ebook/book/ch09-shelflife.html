Realistic shelf life: best-quality ranges

Unopened, stored cool, dry and dark — best-quality windows (USDA / extension guidance)
FoodBest qualityNotes
Canned goods, low-acid (meats, fish, beans, vegetables, soups, stews)2–5 yearsLongest-lived everyday format; can must stay intact
Canned goods, high-acid (tomatoes, fruit, pickles)12–18 monthsAcid slowly attacks the can lining
White rice, dryAbout 2 years typical; much longer sealed oxygen-free and coolBrown rice keeps only 6–12 months (oils)
Dry pasta (no egg)About 2 yearsKeep dry; humidity is the enemy
Quick / rolled oats1–2 yearsAirtight containers help
Dried beans, lentils, split peas1–2 years best qualitySafe well beyond, but older beans take much longer to cook — rotate for fuel reasons alone
Peanut butter, commercial6–9 months unopened; 2–3 months openedPowdered peanut butter keeps longer — check label
Cooking oilsAbout 1 yearSmell before use; rancid oil smells like paint or old crayons
ShorteningFollow label (typically 1–2 years)Keep sealed and cool
Shelf-stable milk cartonsFollow carton date (often 6–12 months)Refrigerate after opening; without power, use single-serve sizes
Nonfat dry milkVaries by packaging — follow labelNitrogen-flushed cans keep longest
Powdered eggsFollow label (commonly 1–3 years)Refrigerate after reconstitution or opening per label
Jerky / meat sticksFollow label (commonly 1–2 years)Sodium-heavy; budget water
Crackers, cereal, granola6–12 monthsStale before unsafe — re-crisp in a dry skillet if fuel allows
Honey, salt, white sugarIndefinite when kept dryHoney crystallizes; warm gently to restore
Freeze-dried / dehydrated commercial productsVaries widely by brand and packaging — years to decadesVerify the specific product's stated life; do not generalize

Shelf life depends on the food, the packaging, and temperature, moisture, oxygen, light and pests. Heat is the accelerator: every storage degree hotter ages food faster. These are best-quality planning windows — always defer to the package in your hand and to current USDA/FDA guidance.
