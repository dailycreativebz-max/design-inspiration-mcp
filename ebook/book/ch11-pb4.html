The Emergency Food PlaybookChapter 11

Extended power outage

Playbook
  • Likely challenge: Days to weeks without power — the core scenario this book is built for; food safety and morale are the grind.
  • Best food format: Full three-layer system in order: fridge → freezer → Layer 1 → Layer 2 → Layer 3.
  • Water concern: Track consumption daily against the Chapter 3 table; refill from treatment if needed.
  • Cooking concern: Rotate methods by fuel budget: no-cook days alternate with hot-water days; one-pot batch days when fuel allows.
  • Storage concern: Appliance thermometers decide the keep/toss calls (Chapter 9).
  • First actions: Note the outage time on the fridge; Doors closed; plan openings in batches; Freeze water jugs if they have not thawed; Start the menu grid from Chapter 7.
  • Avoid: Rationing drinking water; Opening cans 'just in case'; Refrigerating without power and hoping.
  • Stage near the exit: Not evacuation — instead stage fuel, light and the go-bin in case the outage becomes an evacuation.

Short-term grocery shortage

Playbook
  • Likely challenge: Empty shelves of specific staples for days to weeks — not a disaster, but a supply-chain hiccup.
  • Best food format: Your rotating pantry is the answer; this is what weekly rotation buys you.
  • Water concern: Store tap water or buy bottled while it is available; shortages often hit bottled water first.
  • Cooking concern: Normal kitchen; use the meal formula to substitute around the missing item.
  • Storage concern: No change — rotation continues; do not hoard beyond your plan.
  • First actions: Substitute columns in the meal formula (rice out → pasta in); Check two or three stores or order pickup before assuming scarcity; Replenish used items as they appear.
  • Avoid: Panic-buying beyond your documented plan; Buying unfamiliar bulk items you will not eat.
  • Stage near the exit: N/A — keep the car kit stocked regardless.
