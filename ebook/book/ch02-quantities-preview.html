Layer targets at a glance

Per person, approximate planning quantities for shelf-stable food. These are planning starting points, not prescriptions — adjust for appetite, age, climate and activity. Calories and protein are planning aids only; verify nutrition on your actual product labels.

Per-person targets by layer
LayerFood target (per person)Water target
Layer 1 — Ready (72 h)About 9 ready-to-eat meals/snack-meals totaling roughly 4,500–6,000 calories, including 45–60 g protein per day from tuna pouches, nut butters, jerky, beans or shelf milk3 gallons (1 gal/day)
Layer 2 — Transition (days 2–14)About 12–18 hot meals: quick grains, canned meal starters, sauces and seasonings — roughly 1,500–2,000 calories per person per day as the mix widensAdd 7–11 gallons
Layer 3 — Deep (days 7–30+)Dry staples to complete 30 days: rice, pasta, oats, beans, lentils, powdered milk/egg, oil — the full menu grid in Chapter 7 shows how it assemblesAdd through the 30-day total in Chapter 3
Rule of thumb

A typical adult eats about 3 cans of food plus some dry staples per day across two hot meals and breakfast, and a family of four goes through roughly 20–25 cans in the first week alone. Buy toward those numbers gradually — never all at once.
