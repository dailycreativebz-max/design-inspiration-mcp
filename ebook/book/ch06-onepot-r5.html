Salmon Rice Pilaf

Serves 3 · 25 min
High-protein
Ingredients
  • 1 cup instant rice
  • 1 can (14.75 oz) salmon, drained
  • 1 can (10.75 oz) condensed soup (chicken or mushroom)
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/4 cup dehydrated vegetables
  • Black pepper, dried dill (optional)
Preparation
  1. Combine soup, water and vegetables; simmer 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in rice and salmon; cover off heat 5–10 minutes.
  3. Fluff; season.

Subs: Canned tuna or chicken instead of salmon.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 2 cups
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Fish allergy: swap proteins. Salmon bones are edible and calcium-rich — remove if preferred. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Curried Rice and Lentils

Serves 4 · 30 min
High-proteinBudget
Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 cup instant rice
  • 2 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1 tbsp bouillon
  • 1–2 tsp curry powder
  • 1/2 cup canned coconut milk (optional)
  • Salt
Preparation
  1. Simmer lentils in water with bouillon and curry 12–15 minutes.
  2. Stir in rice and coconut milk; cover off heat 5 minutes.
  3. Season.

Subs: Chili powder or taco seasoning instead of curry; skip coconut milk if not stocked.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 3 cups
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Mild for kids by cutting curry powder to 1/2 teaspoon; hot sauce at the table for adults. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Tomato-Tuna Rice Skillet

Serves 3 · 25 min
High-proteinBudget
Ingredients
  • 1 cup instant rice
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 2 cans tuna, drained
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Shelf-stable cheese (optional)
Preparation
  1. Simmer tomatoes, water and seasoning 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in rice; cover off heat 5 minutes.
  3. Fold in tuna; top with cheese if stocked.

Subs: Canned chicken instead of tuna; olives added for adults.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 2 cups
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Fish allergy: use chicken. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Ham and Split Pea Stew

Serves 4 · 45 min
High-protein
Ingredients
  • 1 cup split peas, rinsed
  • 6 cups water or broth
  • 3/4 cup canned ham, diced
  • 1 tsp bouillon
  • Onion powder, black pepper
Preparation
  1. Combine peas, water, bouillon and seasonings.
  2. Simmer 30 minutes; add ham for the final 10 minutes.
  3. Simmer until thick; season.

Subs: Lentils cut cook time roughly in half.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 6 cups
Fuel
High
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Plan this for a day when fuel is plentiful, or finish it with the insulated-pot method. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.
