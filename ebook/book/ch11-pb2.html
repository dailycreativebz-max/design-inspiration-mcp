The Emergency Food PlaybookChapter 11

Winter storm

Playbook
  • Likely challenge: Power lines fail under ice; delivery stops; but cold gives you a backup refrigerator outside (in shade, secured from animals, below 40 °F).
  • Best food format: Hot one-pot meals dominate — morale and body heat both benefit; keep 3 days of no-cook food in case the stove is unusable.
  • Water concern: Pipes can freeze: fill tubs and jugs before the storm. Do not eat snow straight — melt and boil it; it lowers body temperature and may carry contaminants.
  • Cooking concern: Camp stove outdoors or in a ventilated, approved setup only. Carbon monoxide risk spikes in winter — alarms on, generators outside.
  • Storage concern: Unheated garage can be a freezer in this scenario and an oven in summer — use it situationally, with a thermometer.
  • First actions: Fill jugs and tubs; Cook and freeze nothing; instead freeze water; Stock extra propane/butane before the storm; Plan hot breakfasts — oats, cocoa.
  • Avoid: Heating the house with the oven or unvented heaters; running cars in attached garages; any indoor charcoal..
  • Stage near the exit: Car kit (see Car/roadside) plus a bin of hot-meal ingredients near the stove.

Tornado

Playbook
  • Likely challenge: Minutes of warning; possible damage to the house itself, including the pantry; utilities may fail locally.
  • Best food format: A compact go-bin of no-cook food and water that fits in the shelter area itself.
  • Water concern: Small stored amount in the shelter area (1 gallon/person) plus the main supply elsewhere.
  • Cooking concern: Assume nothing until damage is checked; no-cook first, outdoor stove only if the area is safe.
  • Storage concern: Spread supplies across two locations so one destroyed room does not take the whole supply.
  • First actions: Know your shelter spot and put a snack/water bin in it; After: check cans and jars before eating — discard damaged ones; Smell-check for gas before any flame.
  • Avoid: Using flames before checking for gas leaks; Eating from crushed, dented or open cans found in debris.
  • Stage near the exit: The shelter bin stays in the shelter: water bars, pouches, can opener, flashlight.
