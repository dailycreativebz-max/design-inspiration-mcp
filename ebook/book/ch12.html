The household readiness checklist

  1. Everyone in the household knows where the food, water, can opener and flashlight live.
  2. Everyone knows the outdoor cooking spot and the "never indoors" rule.
  3. Emergency contacts are written down (phones die): out-of-state contact, pediatrician, pharmacy, utility, 211.
  4. Medications and dietary-needs list are packed with the go-bag.
  5. Pets have food, water, a leash/carrier and a feeding plan (never share human emergency rations as a diet).
  6. Infants: ready-to-feed formula and feeding supplies staged; formula is never diluted.
  7. One practice meal has been eaten this season, and the results are on the taste-test sheet.
Digital copies

Photograph the completed worksheets and keep copies in cloud storage — paper in a flood is just as lost as food in a flood.
