Thermos Tomato Pasta

Serves 1 · 15 min
Evacuation-readyKid-friendly
Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup small pasta (orzo, stars or broken spaghetti)
  • 1 1/2 cups boiling water
  • 1/3 cup tomato sauce (from a jar or single-serve pouch)
  • Italian seasoning, salt
Preparation
  1. Preheat thermos with boiling water; empty.
  2. Add pasta and fresh boiling water; seal 10–12 minutes.
  3. Stir in tomato sauce and seasonings.

Subs: Couscous or instant rice if pasta won't soften in your thermos; add tuna or chicken.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 2 cups (mostly absorbed)
Fuel
Hot water only
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Test your thermos once at home — cook times vary by brand. Eat within 2 hours of cooking if it cools below 140 degrees F. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Instant Rice and Black Beans

Serves 2 · 10 min
BudgetHigh-protein
Ingredients
  • 1 cup instant rice
  • 1 1/2 cups boiling water or broth
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • Taco seasoning
Preparation
  1. Combine rice and boiling water; cover 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in beans and salsa; cover 2–3 minutes more to warm through.
  3. Season with taco seasoning.

Subs: Canned pinto beans or chickpeas; add shelf-stable cheese if stocked.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 1 1/2 cups (absorbed)
Fuel
Hot water only
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Instant rice instead of regular rice cuts fuel use by about two thirds. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Creamy Tomato Soup with Pasta

Serves 2 · 12 min
Kid-friendlyComfort foodBudget
Ingredients
  • 1 can (10.75 oz) condensed tomato soup
  • 1 soup can of hot water
  • 1/2 cup small pasta
  • 1/2 cup shelf-stable milk
  • Crackers to serve
Preparation
  1. Heat soup and water together to a simmer in one pot.
  2. Add pasta; cook per package (about 8–9 minutes) until tender.
  3. Stir in milk off the heat; serve with crackers.

Subs: Any condensed soup; rice instead of pasta.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 3 cups total
Fuel
Hot water only
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Condensed soups pull double duty as sauce and broth — stock several varieties. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Breakfast Couscous with Cinnamon

Serves 2 · 7 min
BreakfastLow-water
Ingredients
  • 1 cup couscous
  • 1 1/4 cups boiling water or shelf-stable milk
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp dried fruit
  • 2 tbsp nuts or seeds
Preparation
  1. Stir couscous into boiling liquid; cover 5 minutes.
  2. Fluff; stir in sweetener, cinnamon and fruit; top with nuts.

Subs: Instant oats instead of couscous; maple syrup instead of honey.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 1 1/4 cups (absorbed)
Fuel
Hot water only
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Honey should not be given to children under 1 year — see pediatric guidance. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.
