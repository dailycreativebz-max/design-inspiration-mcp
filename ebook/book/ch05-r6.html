Jerky, Nuts and Dried Fruit Plate

Serves 2 · 2 min
High-proteinEvacuation-readyLow-water
Ingredients
  • 2 oz beef or turkey jerky
  • 1/2 cup nuts or seeds
  • 1/3 cup dried fruit
  • Crackers (optional)
Preparation
  1. Arrange on a plate; no preparation.

Subs: Any shelf protein: meat sticks, canned fish, roasted chickpeas.

Method
No-Cook
Water
None
Fuel
No-cook
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Jerky is sodium-heavy; pair each serving with drinking water. Vegetarian: use roasted chickpeas and seeds. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Sardine and White Bean Mash

Serves 2 · 5 min
High-proteinBudget
Ingredients
  • 1 tin sardines
  • 1 can (15 oz) white beans, drained
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Black pepper and lemon juice (bottled, optional)
Preparation
  1. Mash beans and sardines together with oil.
  2. Season; serve on crackers or bread.

Subs: Tuna or salmon instead of sardines; canned chicken for fish-free.

Method
No-Cook
Water
None (draining only)
Fuel
No-cook
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Very high protein per dollar. Fish allergy: use chicken and beans. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

All recipes use shelf-stable ingredients. Approximate water values follow typical package directions — verify on your labels.
