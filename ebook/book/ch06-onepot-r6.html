Alfredo-Style Pasta

Serves 3 · 25 min
Kid-friendlyComfort food
Ingredients
  • 8 oz fettuccine or spaghetti
  • 1 cup shelf-stable milk
  • 3 tbsp shelf-stable cheese sauce or cheese powder
  • 1 tbsp butter powder or oil
  • Black pepper, garlic powder
Preparation
  1. Boil pasta; drain, reserving 1/4 cup water.
  2. Warm milk, cheese and butter powder in the pot.
  3. Toss pasta with sauce, loosening with reserved water.

Subs: Canned chicken added; olive-oil-and-garlic version if dairy-free.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 6 cups for boiling
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes

Storage & safety: Dairy-free: olive oil, garlic powder and a splash of pasta water make a light aglio-e-olio instead. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Spanish Rice with Black Beans

Serves 4 · 25 min
BudgetHigh-protein
Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups instant rice
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes with green chiles
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 3/4 cup water
  • Hot sauce (optional)
Preparation
  1. Combine tomatoes, beans, cumin and water; simmer 8 minutes.
  2. Stir in rice; cover off heat 5–10 minutes.
  3. Fluff; serve with hot sauce.

Subs: Corn stirred in; mild tomatoes for kids.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 2 cups
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: One of the cheapest dinners on this list per serving. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Chili Potato Skillet

Serves 3 · 15 min
Kid-friendlyHigh-proteinComfort food
Ingredients
  • 2 cups instant potato flakes
  • 1 1/4 cups hot water
  • 1 can (15 oz) chili
  • Shelf-stable cheese or crackers
Preparation
  1. Warm chili in the pot.
  2. Fold potato flakes into hot water alongside or in the same pot off heat.
  3. Layer mash and chili; top.

Subs: Canned stew instead of chili; corn on the side.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 1 1/4 cups
Fuel
Low
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Under 15 minutes start to finish — a strong first-hot-meal after an outage. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Pancakes from the Pantry

Serves 3 · 20 min
BreakfastKid-friendlyComfort food
Ingredients
  • 2 cups complete pancake mix (water-only type)
  • 1 1/2 cups water (per mix label)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Syrup, honey or jam to serve
Preparation
  1. Stir mix and water just until combined — lumps are fine.
  2. Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Pour 1/4-cup rounds; flip when bubbles form; cook until golden.

Subs: Add peanut butter on top for protein; mashed canned fruit as a topping.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 1 1/2 cups
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes
2

Storage & safety: Choose 'complete' or 'just add water' mixes for emergencies. Skillet is the only true cooking surface needed. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

All recipes use shelf-stable ingredients. Approximate water values follow typical package directions — verify on your labels.
