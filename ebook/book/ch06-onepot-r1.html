Chili Mac

Serves 4 · 25 min
Kid-friendlyComfort foodHigh-protein
Ingredients
  • 8 oz elbow macaroni
  • 1 can (15 oz) chili with beans
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup water
  • Shelf-stable cheese to top
Preparation
  1. Boil macaroni in one pot per package; drain, reserving 1/2 cup water.
  2. Return pasta to pot; stir in chili, tomato sauce and splashes of reserved water.
  3. Warm through; top with cheese.

Subs: Any short pasta; vegetarian chili; add canned corn.

Pantry Three-Bean Chili

Serves 4 · 25 min
High-proteinBudgetComfort food
Ingredients
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) beans, any mix, drained
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1 packet chili or taco seasoning
  • 1/2 cup water
Preparation
  1. Combine everything in one pot.
  2. Simmer 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. Season to taste.

Subs: Add canned corn, canned chicken or jerky pieces; hot sauce for adults.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 1 cup
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Rinsing beans reduces sodium about 40 percent per USDA-style label math but costs water — your call. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Red Beans and Rice

Serves 4 · 30 min
BudgetHigh-protein
Ingredients
  • 1 1/2 cups instant rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) red or kidney beans, drained
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 tsp bouillon
  • 1 tsp Cajun or chili seasoning
  • 1 1/2 cups water
Preparation
  1. Combine tomatoes, beans, bouillon, seasoning and water; simmer 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in rice; cover and remove from heat 5–10 minutes.
  3. Fluff and serve.

Subs: Canned ham or sausage-style shelf meat stirred in; black beans instead of red.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 2 1/2 cups
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes

Storage & safety: Using instant rice here keeps a classic within low-fuel reach. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Chicken Noodle Soup from Cans

Serves 4 · 25 min
Kid-friendlyComfort foodHigh-protein
Ingredients
  • 2 cans (12.5 oz each) chicken, drained
  • 4 cups water plus 2 cups broth (bouillon)
  • 2 cups egg noodles
  • 1 cup canned mixed vegetables, drained
  • Salt, pepper, dried parsley
Preparation
  1. Bring water and broth to a boil.
  2. Add noodles; cook per package (about 7 minutes).
  3. Add chicken and vegetables; simmer 3–5 minutes; season.

Subs: Rice instead of noodles; canned turkey or ham.

Method
One-Pot
Water
About 6 cups
Fuel
Moderate
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: The single most requested comfort meal during outages — prioritize its ingredients in your list. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.
