Peanut Butter Oatmeal

Serves 1 · 5 min
BreakfastKid-friendlyBudget
Ingredients
  • 1/2 cup quick oats
  • 3/4–1 cup boiling water
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • Pinch of salt
Preparation
  1. Pour boiling water over oats; cover 3 minutes.
  2. Stir in peanut butter, sweetener and salt.

Subs: Powdered milk stirred in for creaminess; raisins or dried fruit instead of sugar.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 1 cup per serving (absorbed)
Fuel
Hot water only

Storage & safety: Follow your oat package's ratio — brands vary. Absorbed water means no dirty dishwater. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Couscous with Chicken and Peas

Serves 2 · 10 min
High-proteinLow-water
Ingredients
  • 1 cup couscous
  • 1 1/4 cups boiling water or broth (bouillon + water)
  • 1 can (12.5 oz) chicken, drained
  • 1/2 cup canned peas, drained
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
Preparation
  1. Stir couscous into boiling water or broth; cover 5 minutes.
  2. Fluff, then fold in chicken, peas and oil.
  3. Season with Italian seasoning, salt and pepper.

Subs: Canned tuna or beans instead of chicken; dehydrated vegetables rehydrated in the same water.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 1 1/4 cups (absorbed)
Fuel
Hot water only
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Couscous is one of the most fuel-efficient grains — it steeps rather than boils. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Upgraded Ramen Bowl

Serves 1 · 8 min
BudgetKid-friendly
Ingredients
  • 1 packet ramen
  • 1 tbsp powdered egg (rehydrated per label) or shelf-stable egg
  • 2 tbsp dehydrated mixed vegetables
  • Half the seasoning packet, to taste
Preparation
  1. Pour boiling water over noodles and dehydrated vegetables in a heat-safe bowl; cover 4 minutes.
  2. Stir in rehydrated egg and half the seasoning packet.

Subs: Add canned chicken for protein; use only part of the seasoning packet to cut sodium.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 2 cups (partly absorbed)
Fuel
Hot water only
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: Ramen seasoning packets are very high in sodium — using half keeps flavor while cutting salt. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

Instant Mash and Gravy Chicken Bowl

Serves 2 · 8 min
Kid-friendlyHigh-proteinComfort food
Ingredients
  • 2 cups instant mashed potato flakes
  • 1 1/2 cups hot water or milk (shelf-stable)
  • 1 jar or packet gravy mix (prepared per label)
  • 1 can (12.5 oz) chicken, drained
  • Black pepper
Preparation
  1. Stir potato flakes into hot water until smooth.
  2. Heat or mix gravy per package; fold in chicken.
  3. Spoon gravy and chicken over the mash.

Subs: Canned ham or turkey instead of chicken; cream-style canned soup as gravy.

Method
Hot-Water
Water
About 1 3/4 cups total (absorbed)
Fuel
Hot water only
Dishes
1

Storage & safety: A high-acceptance comfort meal for kids and older adults with lower chewing needs. Nutrition: check the nutrition label for your chosen brand.

All recipes use shelf-stable ingredients. Approximate water values follow typical package directions — verify on your labels.
