# Commercial Positioning & Sales Copy
### The Emergency Food Playbook — Food Opsec

## One-line positioning

> A practical American emergency-food system for households that want affordable meals, not expensive survival buckets.

## Elevator copy (back cover / product page)

When the power goes out, the freezer has a clock on it — and most "emergency food" advice is either a
fifty-item shopping list you'll never finish or a freeze-dried bucket that costs more than your weekly groceries.

**The Emergency Food Playbook** is the missing middle. It shows an ordinary American household how to build a
real emergency food supply from the same grocery-store food you already eat — then turn it into meals your family
will actually like, with or without electricity.

- **70 shelf-stable recipes** — no-cook, hot-water and one-pot — each with water, fuel and dish counts
- **The Three-Layer Pantry**: what to buy first, later, and never (yet)
- **The 30-Day Menu System** with a power-outage timeline based on USDA food-safety guidance
- **10 disaster playbooks**: hurricanes, floods, winter storms, tornadoes, wildfires, earthquakes, extended
  outages, supply hiccups, income disruptions and car kits
- **The universal meal formula** — improvise dinner from whatever cans are left
- **Rotation that pays for itself**: store what you eat, eat what you store, use one and replace one
- **Printable worksheets**: shopping lists, inventory, rotation tracker, taste-tests, water & fuel calculators,
  dietary substitution charts and a full recipe index

No fear. No fake precision. No miracle foods. Just calm, sourced, budget-first planning — written against
current USDA, FEMA/Ready.gov, CDC, EPA and FDA guidance.

## Bullet blocks (for listings / ads)

**Affordable, not apocalyptic.** Build the whole system across four normal grocery runs — with a $10-per-week
version and a taste-test rule that stops you from stocking food nobody eats.

**High-protein by design.** Sixteen shelf-stable protein sources compared for prep, water, fuel, allergens and
sodium — because a rice-only pantry is a pantry with a hole in it.

**Built for the outage, not the bunker.** Every recipe declares its method, water, fuel and dish count, so you
can cook with a camp stove, a thermos or nothing at all.

**Family-proof.** Kid-friendly, picky-eater-proof, build-your-own meals, plus adaptations for older adults,
allergies, soft-food needs and medically restricted diets (with your clinician).

**U.S.-specific.** U.S. measurements, dollars, products, disasters, and federal sources — nothing lost in
translation.

## Claims we never make (marketing guardrails)

- No guaranteed survival, nutrition, shelf life, cost or disaster protection.
- No "one food saves your life" framing.
- No attacks on commercial freeze-dried brands — objective trade-offs only.
- No medical advice; all medical-adjacent topics route to clinicians and current official guidance.
