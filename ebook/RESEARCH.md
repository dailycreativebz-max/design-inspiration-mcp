accuracy corrections applied against common preparedness-content mistakes, and the content
added beyond the original brief to make the book more complete.

## 3. Accuracy corrections vs. typical preparedness content

Common errors in this genre that this book deliberately avoids:

1. **"Sell-by means unsafe after the date"** — corrected with USDA definitions; dates are quality, not safety (except infant formula).
2. **Universal calorie prescriptions** (e.g., "everyone needs exactly 2,000 cal/day") — replaced with USDA guideline ranges + planning posture, explicitly labeled non-medical.
3. **"Food lasts 25–30 years" blanket claims** — replaced with format-specific best-quality windows and "verify your product" instructions.
4. **Bleach dosing without concentration or contact time** — book gives concentration, clear-vs-cloudy dosing, contact time, and the repeat-once-then-discard rule.
5. **"Keep the freezer door open to check food" / taste-testing** — explicit "never taste," doors closed, verify-don't-taste.
6. **Indoor charcoal/camp-stove advice** — every outdoor-only method is labeled; CO warning appears twice in large format.
7. **Fabricated nutrition numbers** — zero printed macros on recipes; every card carries "check the nutrition label for your chosen brand."
8. **Unsafe leftover advice** — the 2-hour rule is repeated wherever opened/cooked food is discussed; single-serve formats recommended as the enforcement tool.
9. **Home-canning instructions** — none; the book defers to NCHFP and states a discard-don't-taste policy for suspect jars.
10. **Guaranteed-survival marketing language** — none; positioning is household resilience, with explicit no-guarantees disclaimer.

## 5. Content policy decisions

- **No medical diets.** Sodium, diabetes, renal, pregnancy and infant content all route to clinicians; the book provides label tools and swap tables only.
- **No nutrition fabrication.** No recipe prints macros; the label-verification line is mandatory on every card.
- **No brand dependence.** Products are described generically ("canned pork luncheon meat"); trademarks acknowledged on the copyright page.
- **No home-canning procedures.** Deferred to NCHFP; explicit discard policy for suspect jars.
- **Prices are ranges, labeled variable.** No dollar guarantees anywhere.
- **Calm tone enforced.** No apocalypse framing, no "one food saves your life" claims.

## 7. Residual caveats (honest limits)

- Fuel burn rates are intentionally ranges; the fuel calculator worksheet exists because exact numbers depend on the reader's stove, wind and altitude.
- Shelf-life tables are best-quality windows; individual products and storage conditions vary — the book instructs readers to defer to their own labels and current USDA/FDA guidance.
- Freeze-dried cost comparisons are typical market ranges, labeled variable; no brand is named or attacked.
- The book is U.S.-only by design (measurements, products, agencies, scenarios).
